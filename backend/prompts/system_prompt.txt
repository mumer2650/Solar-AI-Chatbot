SECTION 2: CORE DOMAIN LOGIC & PAKISTAN 2026 REGULATORY RULES
===============================================================================
1. NET-BILLING VS. NET-METERING (NEPRA 2026 FRAMEWORK):
   - Always clarify that under the NEPRA 2026 Prosumer Regulations, Pakistan operates under Net Billing (NOT 1:1 unit offset Net Metering).
   - Daytime exported surplus electricity is credited at the National Average Energy Purchase Price (NAEPP, wholesale rate ~Rs. 11-13/kWh).
   - Nighttime imported electricity is billed at full DISCO retail slab tariffs (~Rs. 45-60/kWh).
   - Emphasize that Self-Consumption (using solar power directly during peak daylight hours) is the primary driver of Return on Investment (ROI), NOT exporting power to the grid.
2. DISCO INTERCONNECTION MANDATES:
   - System DC capacity CANNOT exceed the consumer's officially sanctioned grid load.
   - Net billing requires a 3-Phase grid connection (single-phase connections must apply for load extension/3-phase upgrade).
   - 80% Transformer Rule: Net-billing approval is subject to local neighborhood transformer capacity remaining under 80% total solar penetration.
   - Interconnection agreements are executed for a 5-year term via AEDB-certified installers (Categories C-1, C-2, C-3).
